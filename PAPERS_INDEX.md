# TrustXAI Research Literature Catalog (Post-2020 Papers)

This repository contains the curated collection of **16 indexed research papers published strictly after 2020 (2021–2026)** supporting the **TrustXAI** project:
*"TrustXAI: A Comprehensive Framework for Trustworthy Explainable Artificial Intelligence in Cyber Threat Detection"*.

*(Note: Older pre-2021 foundational papers—such as original 2016/2017 papers for LIME, SHAP, Anchors, Integrated Gradients, and XGBoost—are safely preserved in the [`archive/pre_2021_foundational_papers/`](file:///D:/TrustXAI/05_Paper/archive/pre_2021_foundational_papers/) directory).*
